02 / The theory

Targeted resets concentrate policy-advantage signal.

We extend Conservative Policy Iteration with a credit-assignment oracle that targets τ-improvable states. Relative to random-reset CPI, the oracle reduces sample complexity and increases guaranteed per-iteration improvement.

𝒢 Credit-assignment oracle

The oracle conditions reset sampling on states that admit an action with advantage at least τ.

The quantity pπ is the on-policy probability of reaching the improvable set. Conditioning on this set changes the guaranteed advantage from τpπ to τ.

03 / Practical localization

A practical method requires a localizer for improvable states.

The theory asks us to target improvable states: states where the policy admits a strictly better action. Exact credit-assignment oracle access is an idealization. In practice, a localizer identifies improvable states only imperfectly, producing an approximate credit sampler. False discoveries dilute the signal, while missed improvable states shrink the mass on which the update applies.

Example: SRPO

SRPO uses explicit self-localization in place of the credit-assignment oracle.

In the RLVR setting, self-localization of the first erroneous step stands in for the credit-assignment oracle. Given a failed reasoning trajectory, the same policy that generated it is prompted to analyze its own trace. The reset state is the self-determined, verified-correct prefix preceding that error. The localization signal could instead come from a process reward model, step-level environment feedback, or a separate localizer.
